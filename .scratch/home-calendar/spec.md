# Homepage calendar scheduling

Confirmed with the user:

- Show planned workouts and completed sessions on their actual completion dates.
- Unfinished days roll forward automatically each day, shifting the entire remaining schedule and preserving rest days and spacing.
- Completing a rest day offers the next workout early without moving its scheduled date.
- Actually completing that workout early pulls all remaining days forward, preserving spacing.
- Date taps show planned workout previews, completed session results, or a rest-day completion action.

Examples: Monday/Wednesday/Friday workouts become Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday after Monday is missed. Completing Tuesday's rest day leaves Wednesday's workout on Wednesday; completing that workout Tuesday moves the remainder one day earlier.
